## Shared execution work

1. Cargo custody and a physical actor journey: semantic origin/destination, manifest, actual carrier, loading, portal approach, timed route, unloading, seizure without transfer of legal title. Use for arms first; no command creates cargo or declares victory.
2. Delivered institutional instructions: local knowledge at issuer, courier/official movement, receiving actor, enforcement. Reuse for protection, guard rotation and stand-down; issuance is never the physical endpoint.
3. Actor operations: pursuit/capture/escort, defense/attack, displacement/rescue, mobilisation and civilian response. Bind authored actors and resources; no replacement actor spawned on demand.
4. Powered/ecological actor evolution: physical activation, affected people/creatures, movement, exposure and persistent aftermath.

Existing guards: LOS, regional presence, no offline catch-up, paused decision calendar, semantic replay, property ownership and save continuity stay mandatory. Geometry stays replaceable blockout.
