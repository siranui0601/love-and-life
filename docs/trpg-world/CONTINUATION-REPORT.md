## Limits and remaining issues

- P0: No known data-loss or immediate-damage regression in the exercised paths. This does not certify every historical save/content combination: only explicit known versions are supported.
- P1: full world crisis migration and multiple complete causal worldlines; richer institutional adjudication and downstream event conditions; finite resources/employer money/foraging limits; nested owned objects and private document access; runtime production of conflicting credit claims and richer obligation decisions; shared temporal affordances beyond discrete acts; rescue replanning for changing obstacles and unavailable services; directional hit volume/projectile simulation.
- P2: large-world planning budgets and long-session fact/plan retention, training teacher availability/mastery assessment, presentation of action outcomes, final motion/animation/art and balancing.
- The rescue-chain test uses a disclosed initial collapsed-save fixture. The relationship attribution and planner-resource-invalidation tests are focused state-fixture tests. They are not represented as complete player-driven worldlines.
- The rumor chain test uses actual NPC proximity and the runtime NPC conversation/knowledge propagation loop. The recipient did not witness the original action and replans only after hearing it.
- The browser smoke was run on the recovered 3D interface. Final combat hit-trail changes passed the client build; no claim is made of a complete visual combat playthrough.
